T0 WHOM IT MAY CONCERN:
Dear Sir:
I hereby highly recommend Miss Deirdre Hurst
(Lbs. E. du Prey) as a qualified actress,
teacher and director.
During the years
Miss Hurst was associated with me,
I was able
to discover her. exceptional abilities and her
skilfull approach to theatrical work.
Because
of this,
it gave me great pleasure to mention
Miss Hurst as a qualified teacher, in the pre-
face of my book on the Technique of Acting.
I feel sure that any- position that she feels
she wants to undertake will be fulfilled to
the best of her ability.
Sincerely,
