To all Directorsx\ Always begin the rehearsal with
a short exorcise which will unify everyone.
The closer your
relationship is,
the better the rehearsal will be.
To be
able to love someone who is a stranger to us is a wonder, and
that is the basis of our art.
Try to find exercises which
will awaken this uniting.
