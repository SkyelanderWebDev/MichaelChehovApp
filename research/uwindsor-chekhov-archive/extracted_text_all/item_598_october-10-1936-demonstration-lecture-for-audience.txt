COctober 10,
1936
Demonstration Lesson for Audience
DEMONRSTRATION LESSCN :
Firat you must concentrate on the audience — project
your power to the audience — send out your love to the audience.
Exerciges:
1.
Concentrate on our audience.
2.
Concentrate on the spot on the wall.
3.
Actor‘s march — walking and running in a circle.
4.
 Staccato and legato exercises.
5
Fighting groups.
Then one fighting group and
one juggler group, with a middle group linking
the outside groups.
6.
Group divided into two lines approaching each
other, clasping hands and saying,
"I‘m not sure
that I can, but I will try to doit."
Repeat in
different tempos.
